# Consent-governed product analytics (PR #143).
-- New: Personal Settings has a Data & communication section with two separate choices: product analytics and personalized help, and offers and tips by email. Account › Data disclosure (202-account/disclosure.php) says what each sends to Prosper202 and what it never sends. Accounts in the EU or UK are asked once before any product analytics is sent; elsewhere product analytics is on until you switch it off, from the first sign-in that shows the account is outside the EU and UK; until then it is held. The location is read from the address that connects, or behind Cloudflare from the visitor address Cloudflare passes on, never from a forwarding header a browser can set, so only an install behind another proxy or CDN keeps product analytics held until it is switched on in Personal Settings. The analytics an older version queued before these choices existed is deleted by the upgrade, never sent. The EU/UK prompt stays open until your choice is saved. Switching it off deletes the analytics this install had not yet sent, and nothing captured before then is sent if you switch it back on. Each change of either choice is sent to Prosper202 again, hourly, until Prosper202 confirms it stored it. Sign-ins, new accounts and support delivery are always sent, as they are needed to run your account.
app: New: Choose separately whether Prosper202 receives product analytics and whether it emails you offers and tips (Personal Settings › Data & communication).
-- New: With product analytics on, Prosper202 learns which setup steps an account has reached: a campaign, campaign category, traffic source or tracker made on the pages, with the CLI, through p202 mcp or a connected AI app; a redirector, goal, landing page, text ad, mobile app or sync job made with the CLI, p202 mcp or a connected AI app; a click filter setting saved or address added; a payment notification or other integration key saved; an LTV integration; a paired Landing Page Optimizer; a connected AI app. It also learns which of the CLI, a connected AI app or another API client an account uses (at most once a day each), and a 30-day summary of the account's traffic and offers, with offer URLs cut to their address, plain path words, query names, tracking macros and short ids (sign-in details, fragments and every other value are taken out) and LTV figures only as account totals. A connected AI app is named only as Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Gemini or other. Click details, visitor addresses and your customers' data are never sent.
-- Changed: Page events and attributes sent by Prosper202Messenger('trackEvent') and ('update') are recorded only for an account with product analytics on.
