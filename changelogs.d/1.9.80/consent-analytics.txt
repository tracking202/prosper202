# Consent-governed product analytics (PR #143).
-- New: Usage data is opt-in. Nothing about how an account uses Prosper202 is recorded for sending or sent to Tracking202 until that user turns it on, wherever they are; a choice never made is off, and the box starts unticked. Personal Settings has a Data & communication section with two separate choices: sharing how you use Prosper202 (so the tips, guides and announcements you get fit how you use it), and offers and tips by email. Each user is asked once, with "Yes, share usage" and "No thanks" side by side, and never again whichever they pick or if they leave it. The question, the box and Usage Data (202-account/disclosure.php) list what is sent and what is never sent. Only what is recorded after a yes is sent: the analytics an older version queued is kept by the upgrade but never sent, and nothing recorded before a yes is sent after it. Switching it off deletes the usage data this install had not yet sent, and nothing captured before then is sent if you switch it back on. A change of either choice is sent to Tracking202 only when one of them is on or Tracking202 has already heard from that user, and then again hourly until Tracking202 confirms it stored it. Sign-ins and new accounts are no longer sent for a user who has not opted in, and the sign-in no longer looks up where the user signs in from.
app: New: Sharing how you use Prosper202 is off until you turn it on (Personal Settings › Data & communication). You're asked once, and either answer is one click.
-- New: With product analytics on, Prosper202 learns which setup steps an account has reached: a campaign, campaign category, traffic source or tracker made on the pages, with the CLI, through p202 mcp or a connected AI app; a redirector, goal, landing page, text ad, mobile app or sync job made with the CLI, p202 mcp or a connected AI app; a click filter setting saved or address added; a payment notification or other integration key saved; an LTV integration; a paired Landing Page Optimizer; a connected AI app. It also learns which of the CLI, a connected AI app or another API client an account uses (at most once a day each), and a 30-day summary of the account's traffic and offers, with offer URLs cut to their address, plain path words, query names, tracking macros and short ids (sign-in details, fragments and every other value are taken out) and LTV figures only as account totals. A connected AI app is named only as Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Gemini or other. Click details, visitor addresses and your customers' data are never sent.
-- Changed: Page events and attributes sent by Prosper202Messenger('trackEvent') and ('update') are recorded only for an account with product analytics on.
