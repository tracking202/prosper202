# Consent-governed product analytics (PR #143).
-- New: Personal Settings has a Data & communication section with two separate choices: product analytics and personalized help, and offers and tips by email. Account › Data disclosure (202-account/disclosure.php) says what each sends to Prosper202 and what it never sends. Accounts in the EU or UK are asked once before any product analytics is sent; elsewhere product analytics is on until you switch it off, from the first sign-in that shows the account is outside the EU and UK; until then it is held. Switching it off deletes the analytics this install had not yet sent. Sign-ins, new accounts and support delivery are always sent, as they are needed to run your account.
app: New: Choose separately whether Prosper202 receives product analytics and whether it emails you offers and tips (Personal Settings › Data & communication).
-- New: With product analytics on, Prosper202 learns which setup steps an account has reached (a first campaign, traffic source, tracker, redirector, goal, mobile app, click filter change, payment notification or LTV integration, a paired Landing Page Optimizer, a connected AI app) whether they were made on the pages, with the CLI, through p202 mcp or a connected AI app, and which of those an account uses (at most once a day each). It also learns a 30-day summary of the account's traffic and offers, with offer URLs stripped of anything personal and LTV figures only as account totals. Click details, visitor addresses and your customers' data are never sent.
-- Changed: Page events and attributes sent by Prosper202Messenger('trackEvent') and ('update') are recorded only for an account with product analytics on.
