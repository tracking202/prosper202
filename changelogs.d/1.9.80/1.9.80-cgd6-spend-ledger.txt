# CGD6: the spend ledger, and reports that read what the ad platform billed.
-- New: A spend ledger records what each ad platform billed per ad per hour (impressions, the platform's clicks and spend in the account's currency), written through POST /api/v3/spend-ledger (scope spend-ledger:write, role permission access_to_update_section, ?dry_run=1 to preview) and read through GET /api/v3/spend-ledger by day in the account's time zone or by hour. Sending an hour again replaces it (the last write of an hour wins), so a puller can re-read the hours whose spend is still settling; one ad id names one ad, so an ad already recorded under another platform or ad account is refused; spend in a currency other than the account's is refused by name.
app: New: A spend ledger keeps what each ad platform billed per ad per hour, for the reports to use as cost.
-- Changed: The REST API reports (summary, breakdown, timeseries, daypart, weekpart, groups and a rotator's stats) cost a click at its share of the hour's spend when the spend ledger has its ad and hour: the ad is the click's traffic source variable with parameter ad_id. Every other click keeps its CPC. Such a report carries spend_ledger, which says how far the spend data reaches and how much spend in the window no click carries (unallocated, never spread onto other clicks). An account with no ledger rows reads every report exactly as before.
app: Changed: Reports use the spend an ad platform reported for the clicks it matches, and say how far that spend data reaches.
-- Changed: The report pages in the browser (Overview's tables and chart, Group Overview, Breakdown, Day and Week Parting, the Analyze reports, Custom Variables and Publishers) cost clicks from the spend ledger as the REST reports do, and show above their figures how far the spend data reaches and how much spend in the window no click carries. An account with no ledger rows in the window sees every page exactly as before.
app: Changed: Overview, Group Overview, Breakdown and the Analyze pages show the same ad platform spend as cost, with a note above the figures.
-- New: `p202 spend import <file|->` records ad platform spend from a JSON file of rows (a list or {"rows": [...]}, up to 2000; --dry-run previews) and `p202 spend list` reads the spend ledger per day or hour (--period, --time-from/--time-to, --interval, --platform, --limit).
