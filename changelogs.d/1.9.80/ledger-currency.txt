-- New: every conversion keeps the currency its amount is in (202_conversion_logs.currency), written when the conversion is recorded, so changing the account's currency no longer relabels the conversions already there: they stay in the currency they were recorded in, unconverted, and new ones are recorded in the new currency. The conversion log, the click's conversions panel, GET /conversions, GET /clicks/{id}/conversions, `p202 click conversions`, the offline conversion files and the GA4 and Google Ads destinations show or send each conversion's own currency. Reports add each click's value and carry no currency of their own, so when part of a period's revenue is not in the account's currency the Overview, Group Overview, Breakdown, Day Parting, Week Parting, Rotator Breakdown and Analyze pages say how much of each other currency the period holds above their figures, every /reports answer and GET /conversions/match-methods list it as `other_currencies`, `p202` prints it under a report, and `p202 report summary --profiles` does not add the money of a profile whose own revenue is in more than one currency. Personal settings says what a change of currency does to earlier conversions before it is saved and after, and PUT /users/{id}/preferences answers it as `currency_change`. The upgrade records the account's currency on the conversions already there, except for an account whose currency was changed before (its campaigns keep the payout they had under the earlier one) or that has a campaign paid in another currency: which of its conversions were recorded under which currency cannot be known, so they are shown with no currency recorded, counted apart in the reports' notice and left out of offline conversion files (`no_currency`), and Personal settings › Account currency lets the owner record the currency they were in.
app: New: each conversion keeps the currency it was recorded in. Changing your account's currency no longer relabels earlier conversions, and reports say when a period holds more than one currency.
-- New: a postback or pixel that sends its own amount on a campaign paid in another currency than the account's has that amount converted into the account's currency, as paid 1.9.56 did, at an exchange rate Prosper202 keeps (202_exchange_rates): the cron job fetches the rate for each pair of currencies the campaigns need once a day, and saving such a campaign or changing the account's currency fetches it at once, so no postback waits on the rate service. The conversion keeps the amount and currency that were sent beside the converted amount (`stated_amount`, `stated_currency`). With no rate kept for the pair, or one more than seven days old, the amount is recorded as sent, in the campaign's currency, and shown as such everywhere. A refund of a converted sale is converted at the rate its sale was, so a full refund takes back exactly what was recorded. A ClickBank amount (USD) on an account that counts in another currency is recorded as USD.
