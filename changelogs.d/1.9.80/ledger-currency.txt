-- New: every conversion keeps the currency its amount is in (202_conversion_logs.currency), written when the conversion is recorded, so changing the account's currency no longer relabels the conversions already there: they stay in the currency they were recorded in, unconverted, and new ones are recorded in the new currency. The conversion log, the click's conversions panel, GET /conversions, GET /clicks/{id}/conversions, `p202 click conversions`, the offline conversion files and the GA4 and Google Ads destinations show or send each conversion's own currency. Reports add each click's value and carry no currency of their own, so when part of a period's revenue is not in the account's currency the Overview, Group Overview, Breakdown, Day Parting, Week Parting, Rotator Breakdown and Analyze pages say how much of each other currency the period holds above their figures, every /reports answer and GET /conversions/match-methods list it as `other_currencies`, `p202` prints it under a report, and `p202 report summary --profiles` does not add the money of a profile whose own revenue is in more than one currency. Personal settings says what a change of currency does to earlier conversions before it is saved and after, and PUT /users/{id}/preferences answers it as `currency_change`. The upgrade records the account's currency on the conversions already there, except for an account whose currency was changed before (its campaigns keep the payout they had under the earlier one) or that has a campaign paid in another currency: which of its conversions were recorded under which currency cannot be known, so they are shown with no currency recorded, counted apart in the reports' notice and left out of offline conversion files (`no_currency`), and Personal settings › Account currency lets the owner record the currency they were in, after which what a destination was not sent for want of one goes out. The upgrade tells these accounts by the sign a change of currency left on their campaigns, which is not complete: an account whose change left none (every campaign already in the new currency, campaigns paid only by the amounts their postbacks send, or no campaign) has its earlier conversions labelled with its current currency, and one with a campaign paid in another currency that never changed currency has them left unrecorded. So Personal settings › Account currency always offers to say which currency the conversions before a date were in (and, under Advanced, from a date): it changes only the conversions the upgrade labelled or left unrecorded, and their refunds, marked as such (202_conversion_logs.currency_basis), never one recorded with its own currency, and can be said again. From 1.9.80 each change of the account's currency is recorded when it is made (202_currency_changes: when, from and to), from Personal settings and PUT /users/{id}/preferences alike, and Personal settings lists them. A report download and the Overview chart, which are read without their page, say the same in themselves: every download row ends with `Currency` and `Other currencies in period`, the Group Overview download says it in its heading and the chart in its subtitle. A refund from Shopify, PayKickstart, JVZoo or Zaxaa is held to the currency its sale recorded, not to the account's currency of the day, so a sale recorded before the account's currency changed is refunded in the currency it was sold in; one stated in another currency than its sale's is refused. So is a Shopify refund recorded by hand (Setup › Inbound Log, the API and `p202 conversion shopify-refunds record`): its amount is stated in its sale's currency, which the list gives as `amount_currency`. A conversion whose amount is from another time takes the currency of that time from the account's currency history (202_currency_changes, which also records each owner's statement and where the history begins at the upgrade): one that takes its click's payout is in the currency of the click's time, and one dated in the past (a replay, an import) in the currency of its own; where the history does not reach, it takes the account's at the upgrade and is marked for the owner to correct (currency_basis). A refund of a sale with no currency recorded is recorded beside it and takes the sale's currency when the owner records it, instead of waiting for a change of the account's currency. An account with no preferences row records its conversions with no currency, as the upgrade leaves its earlier ones. Analyze › Publishers says the period's other currencies above its figures, and its download ends every row with the two currency columns. The conversion.recorded webhook payload carries the payout's currency.
app: New: each conversion keeps the currency it was recorded in. Changing your account's currency no longer relabels earlier conversions, and reports say when a period holds more than one currency. If you changed your account's currency before this version, say when on Personal settings and your earlier conversions take the currency they were in.
-- New: a postback or pixel that sends its own amount on a campaign paid in another currency than the account's has that amount converted into the account's currency, as paid 1.9.56 did, at an exchange rate Prosper202 keeps (202_exchange_rates): the cron job fetches the rate for each pair of currencies the campaigns need once a day, and saving such a campaign or changing the account's currency fetches it at once, so no postback waits on the rate service. The conversion keeps the amount and currency that were sent beside the converted amount (`stated_amount`, `stated_currency`). With no rate kept for the pair, or one more than seven days old, the amount is recorded as sent, in the campaign's currency, and shown as such everywhere. A refund of a converted sale is converted at the rate its sale was, so a full refund takes back exactly what was recorded. A ClickBank amount (USD) on an account that counts in another currency is recorded as USD. The rate request holds the two currency codes and a reference amount only, is never redirected, and is given 10 seconds, a refresh run 30. Since 1.9.57 to 1.9.79 recorded such amounts as sent, the upgrade marks the campaigns paid in another currency (202_aff_campaigns.aff_campaign_currency_notice), and Personal settings lists them with one button to switch them all to the account's currency (their networks report in it now) and one to keep converting. A campaign paid only by its postbacks' amounts (payout 0) is paid in its own currency too, through a change of the account's currency; the upgrade keeps such a campaign from before 1.9.80 in its currency, and lists it on Personal settings, on an account that shows an earlier change of currency, and gives it the account's currency elsewhere, as its amounts were recorded. The rates are refreshed for deleted campaigns too, and a page or API call that saves a campaign or the account's currency refreshes only its account's pairs, within 12 seconds.
