-- Changed: A conversion now goes by one event id on every channel: its transaction id, or P202-<conversion id> when it has none, and for a conversion that replaced earlier ones the id of the first. The Meta Purchase that a Bot202 Facebook Pixel Assistant pixel fires uses it (for a payable goal too), as the server-side destinations always did, so Meta counts a sale it receives from both once; as with GA4, a replacing conversion with a new amount is then not counted again. The [[transactionid]] token in traffic source pixels and postbacks is unchanged. Destinations can now be given the visitor's address and the click's page, for platforms that need them: the address only when the destination sends user data, the campaign captures identity, the click's latest visit did not refuse consent, and the address was stored unmasked and public; the page is the landing page (or the tracking link the click came through) without its query, fragment, user:password@ or ;path parameters. What GA4 and Google Ads receive is unchanged.
