-- Changed: A conversion now goes by one event id on every channel: its transaction id, or P202-<conversion id> when it has none, and for a conversion that replaced earlier ones the id of the first. The Meta Purchase that a Bot202 Facebook Pixel Assistant pixel fires uses it, as the server-side destinations always did, so Meta counts a sale it receives from both once. The [[transactionid]] token in traffic source pixels and postbacks is unchanged. Destinations can now be given the visitor's address (only when the destination sends user data and the address was stored unmasked) and the click's landing page (or the tracking link it came through), for platforms that need them; what GA4 and Google Ads receive is unchanged.
