# Shared goals: one goal counting across campaigns, or on every campaign.
-- New: A goal can count on several campaigns. Any goal of the account, another campaign's own goal included, is set on a campaign with PUT /api/v3/goals/{id}/campaigns/{campaign_id} (p202 goal campaign set), so a funnel's goals are defined once and counted on each campaign that feeds it; payable: false (--not-payable) counts it there without paying, written as a tracked outcome and conversion row. An account goal can count on every campaign instead, campaigns made later included (PUT /api/v3/goals/{id}/every-campaign, p202 goal campaign set <id> all), tracked unless a campaign pays for it. A share counts events received from the moment it is made; changing it between paid and tracked keeps that start, and removing it keeps what it counted. Setup › Campaigns lists the goals shared with a campaign, paid or tracked. Upgrading adds 202_goals.every_campaign_since and 202_campaign_goals.payable; every existing goal and campaign payout is unchanged.
