-- New: a third money-loss alert, Postback refusals jumped. Once an hour, with the other alerts, the cron job counts each account's refused postbacks and pixels in the inbound log (Setup › Inbound Log) and tells the account when the last hour holds at least 10 refusals and at least 5 times its average refusals per hour over the 24 hours before: a network that changed its postback format, a rotated token or signature secret, or a campaign deleted under a live offer. The message names the hour's most frequent refusal reasons. It is about the account rather than a campaign and is sent at most once a day while the jump lasts, through the same channels and settings as the other alerts, and to webhooks subscribed to the new event alert.postback_refusals_spiked (or to every event). Requests the install could not attribute to an account are not counted.
