-- Fixed: The Bot202 Facebook Pixel Assistant from paid 1.9.56 did nothing after upgrading: its per-landing-page settings were kept in the database but never shown or used, and a traffic source pixel of the type "Bot202 Facebook Pixel Assistant" never fired. Setup › Landing Pages shows and saves the settings again for simple landing pages (on/off, dynamic EPV, content name, content type, outbound click event). The landing page script fires the Meta Pixel for visits from a traffic source with such a pixel: PageView and ViewContent on the page, and the chosen event (ViewContent or AddToCart) once on the click out to the offer, carrying the source's earnings per outbound click over the last 7 days when dynamic EPV is on. A conversion fires a Meta Pixel Purchase with its payout, in the account's currency, with its transaction id as the event id. Nothing fires for a visitor the privacy setting holds back or who refused consent (p202_consent=0, p202.consent(false)). The traffic source form stores the Pixel ID from pasted Meta Pixel code and refuses a code without one. Settings saved on 1.9.56 are used as they are.
