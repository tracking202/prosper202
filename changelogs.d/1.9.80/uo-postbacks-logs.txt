-- New: A warning when conversion postbacks queued for your traffic sources are not being sent. They are sent right after the answer under PHP-FPM or LiteSpeed and otherwise, and on every retry, by the cron job; with no cron job running they wait and the traffic source never hears of the conversion. When one has been due for 15 minutes and no cron job or AutoCron run has been recorded since, Home, the Account Overview and Account › Settings say how many are waiting, for how long, and to set up the cron job or turn on AutoCron. 202-cronjobs/health.php, GET /api/v3/system/cron (traffic_source_postbacks) and p202 system cron say the same.
