# 1P21: ChatGPT Ads spend pulled hourly into the spend ledger.
-- New: ChatGPT Ads spend sync. For a ChatGPT Ads connection whose sync is turned on (Setup › Traffic Sources' ChatGPT Ads spend card, p202 spend sync enable, or PUT /api/v3/spend-ledger/sync/{id}), Prosper202 reads what OpenAI billed per ad per hour every hour and writes it to the spend ledger, so reports cost the clicks carrying each ad at its real spend. The first run reads the 30 days OpenAI keeps hourly; later runs re-read the last 72 hours, since spend settles after the clicks. It is off until turned on, an ad account billed in another currency than the Prosper202 account is refused by name, and OpenAI's rate limit or a refused key is waited out (a new key ends the wait). A run reads a day at a time and the next run goes on where one stopped, so a long first read or an outage loses nothing OpenAI still keeps. Spend with more than six decimals is rounded half up; a row the ledger still cannot take keeps the run not complete and is shown on the Traffic Sources card until those hours are read cleanly. p202 spend sync run answers OpenAI's limit, outage and unusable answers as the ad connections do (exit 7 or 4), and a refused key as one to replace. Each run also reads the conversions OpenAI attributes to those ads per day, shown beside the conversions Prosper202 recorded for clicks carrying the same ads (GET /api/v3/spend-ledger/conversions, p202 spend conversions, the Traffic Sources card). p202 spend list shows the spend per ad by day or hour.
app: New: ChatGPT Ads spend can be pulled every hour into Prosper202, so reports show what OpenAI billed instead of a typed CPC.

They load through get_more_tools, so the list an AI app reads at the start is no longer.
