# 1P21: ChatGPT Ads spend pulled hourly into the spend ledger.
-- New: ChatGPT Ads spend sync. For a ChatGPT Ads connection whose sync is turned on (Setup › Traffic Sources' ChatGPT Ads spend card, p202 ad-spend sync enable, or PUT /api/v3/spend-ledger/sync/{id}), Prosper202 reads what OpenAI billed per ad per hour every hour and writes it to the spend ledger, so reports cost the clicks carrying each ad at its real spend. The first run reads the 30 days OpenAI keeps hourly; later runs re-read the last 72 hours, since spend settles after the clicks. It is off until turned on, an ad account billed in another currency than the Prosper202 account is refused by name, and OpenAI's rate limit is waited out. Each run also reads the conversions OpenAI attributes to those ads per day, shown beside the conversions Prosper202 recorded for clicks carrying the same ads (GET /api/v3/spend-ledger/conversions, p202 ad-spend conversions, the Traffic Sources card). p202 ad-spend shows the spend per ad by day or hour.
