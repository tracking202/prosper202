-- New: Clicks from ChatGPT ads keep OpenAI's click id, oppref, as a seventh ad click id beside gclid, gbraid, wbraid, ttclid, msclkid and fbclid. Direct links (dl.php), Google Ads parallel tracking (pt.php) and simple and advanced landing pages record it from the URL (the landing page script sends it on; dynamic content reads it as t202OPPREF); it is kept as sent in 202_google.oppref, up to 1,000 characters and compared exactly (case included). [[oppref]] fills in campaign, postback and pixel URLs and PurLinks; it is not passed on to the offer unless the offer URL names its token, as the gclid is not. A global postback (gpb.php) with no SubID that sends oppref back finds its click, as it does for the other ids, and an account's own parameter names may not be oppref, which is read under its own name. GET /clicks and /clicks/{id}, `p202 click list`/`click get` and the Visitors download show it, null for none. The upgrade adds the column and fills the ad click id lookup with it; until the upgrade has run, a click is recorded with every other id and without its oppref, which the error log says.
