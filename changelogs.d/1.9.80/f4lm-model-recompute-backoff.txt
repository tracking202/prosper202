-- Fixed: A model change whose recompute failed on every run no longer stops multi-touch attribution for the whole install. The worker took model changes in the order they were made, and one that failed every time was first in every run, so the models after it, new conversions and the reports' rollup were not credited again until it went through. Now a model whose recompute fails is recorded with its error, passed, and tried again on the next run, then after a minute, doubling to an hour; the other models and new conversions keep being credited meanwhile, and a conversion not yet re-credited keeps its previous credits. GET /api/v3/attribution/queue (p202 attribution queue) shows the failing models in models_failing and model_rows with each one's last error, the Attribution page says how many are failing, editing the model again starts it afresh, and php 202-cronjobs/attribution-worker.php --retry-now tries them at once.
