-- Fixed: Update Subids took only bare subids after upgrading from paid 1.9.56, so the payout, transaction id and time on each line, and the page's payout and time zone inputs, were gone. Lines are read as SUBID, PAYOUT, TRANSACTION ID, TIMESTAMP again (every field after the subid optional), with a default payout and a time zone (your account's unless you choose another), and recorded through the conversion ledger: a line with a transaction id records that sale once, so a click can carry several sales. A line that cannot be read (a currency sign, thousands separator or decimal comma in the payout, a negative amount, a date that does not exist, is in the future or is before its click) is named and nothing in the list is recorded, instead of being skipped. POST /api/v3/conversions/subids reads the same lines and takes payout and timezone.
app: Fixed: Update Subids reads payout, transaction id and time on each line again, with a default payout and time zone.
-- Fixed: Setup > Campaigns had lost the per-campaign currency paid 1.9.56 offered, so a campaign paid in another currency could only be entered in the account's. The currency field is back: a payout in another currency is kept as entered and converted into the account's currency at the day's rate when the campaign is saved (if no rate can be had, nothing is saved and the page says so), conversions at the campaign payout record the converted amount, and the campaign list shows both. A postback that reports its own amount is still recorded as sent.
app: Fixed: Campaigns can be paid in another currency again; the payout is converted to your account currency when you save.

Personal settings and PUT /api/v3/users/{id}/preferences now ask it to convert from the campaign's currency into the new one. Campaigns already re-priced by an earlier change are not corrected.
