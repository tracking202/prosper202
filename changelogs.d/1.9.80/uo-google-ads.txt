-- New: An install upgraded from paid 1.9.56 whose Google Ads schedule still fetches the retired feed (tracking202/static/feeds/google/offlineconversions/?cn=…&ac=…) is told so. Each fetch of the old address is noted (202_legacy_google_feed_fetches, made by the 1.9.80 upgrade step), and Setup › Offline Conversions opens with "Your old Google Ads feed": each schedule by the conversion action and campaign its address carries, and when Google Ads last tried it. Move it over saves that conversion action as the campaign's Google name (the account's name for every other campaign, for ac=--) and makes its fetch URL in the Uploads template format for the last 90 days, shown once to paste into the schedule; it asks before replacing a name the campaign already has. Until then the page carried a static note and the values had to be typed again. While a schedule waits to be moved, Home and Account Overview say the uploads have stopped and link to it.
app: New: If Google Ads is still fetching your old paid 1.9.56 conversion feed, Setup › Offline Conversions shows it and moves it over with one button.
-- New: Setup › Destinations picks a Google Ads destination's account and conversion action from your Google Ads account instead of asking for their IDs. Add Google Ads takes a name, the developer token and the OAuth client, and goes straight to Google to connect; back on the page you choose from the accounts the connected Google user can open (with the manager each is reached through, which becomes the login customer ID) and from that account's enabled conversion actions that take uploaded clicks. A working destination has "change account or action". The IDs can still be typed under Advanced, and the CLI and API take them as before; a destination made without them reads status needs_account, and its conversions wait, using no tries, until both are set. The names Google gives the picked account and action are kept (settings.labels) and shown in the list beside the IDs. The API lists them (GET /destinations/{id}/google-ads/accounts and /conversion-actions) and sets them by name (POST /destinations/{id}/google-ads/pick), and the CLI has p202 destination accounts, conversion-actions and pick; destination create --type google_ads no longer needs --customer-id or --conversion-action.
app: New: Google Ads destinations: pick the account and conversion action from your Google Ads account instead of pasting IDs.
