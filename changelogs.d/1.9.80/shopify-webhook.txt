-- Fixed: Shopify order webhooks that paid 1.9.56 installs sent to the global postback (tracking202/static/gpb.php) recorded nothing after upgrading. They are recorded again, now only when they verify: each webhook's X-Shopify-Hmac-Sha256 signature is checked against the Shopify webhook secret stored under Account › API integrations › Shopify, and one that does not verify, or arrives before any secret is stored, is refused and logged with the reason. A paid order (orders/paid, or orders/create for an order already paid) is recorded once under its order id at its total_price, on the click its t202subid line item property names, and refunds/create takes back what the refund returned. A verified webhook that cannot be recorded is logged and answered 200, so Shopify does not retry it and drop the subscription. The webhooks can also be pointed at the new tracking202/static/shopify.php.
