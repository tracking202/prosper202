-- New: Money-loss alerts (Account › Personal settings › Money-loss alerts, off until an account turns them on). Once an hour the cron job checks each of the account's campaigns and tells it, once per incident, when a campaign that converts steadily (10 conversions in the last 7 days by default) has had no conversion for several times its usual gap between conversions (5 by default, at least an hour) while real clicks keep arriving at several times its usual clicks per conversion, so a postback or pixel that broke quietly is found within hours rather than at the next look; and when a campaign's cost today, in the account's time zone, passes a daily cap the account sets with no conversion today (at most once a day). Alerts go to the account's own Slack incoming webhook, its email address (through the server's mail()), and its webhooks subscribed to the new events alert.conversions_stopped and alert.spend_without_conversions. The next conversion re-arms the first alert. 202_conversion_logs gains an index on (user_id, conv_time) for the check, added by the 1.9.80 upgrade step.
