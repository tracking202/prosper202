-- New: Money-loss alerts, on for every account by default, including accounts that existed before the upgrade (Account › Personal settings › Money-loss alerts turns them off). Once an hour the cron job checks each account's campaigns. It tells the account once per incident when a campaign that converts steadily (10 conversions in the last 7 days by default) has had no conversion for several times its usual gap between conversions (5 by default, at least an hour) while real clicks keep arriving at several times its usual clicks per conversion, so a postback or pixel that broke quietly is found within hours rather than at the next look. It also alerts when a campaign's cost today, in the account's time zone, passes a daily cap the account sets with no conversion today, at most once a day (no cap is set by default). Alerts go by email to the account's own address (through the server's mail()), to Slack only where the account has set an incoming webhook, and to the account's webhooks that subscribe to the new events alert.conversions_stopped and alert.spend_without_conversions. An account with none of these gets nothing. Every email and Slack message says how to turn the alerts off. The next conversion re-arms the first alert, and an alert that no channel could take is tried at most three times. 202_conversion_logs gains an index on (user_id, conv_time) for the check, added by the 1.9.80 upgrade step.
