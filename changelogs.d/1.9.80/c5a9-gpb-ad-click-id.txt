-- New: A global postback (tracking202/static/gpb.php) whose SubID was not filled in can still record its conversion. When subid (and sid) is empty or arrives as the network's unfilled macro and nothing else ([[subid]], {aff_sub}, {{name}}, ${name}, [name], [=SID=], #s2#, %subid1%, <name>), the postback was answered "SubID not found" and the sale was lost. It now finds the click by an ad click id the postback sends back: gclid, gbraid, wbraid, msclkid, fbclid or ttclid under its own name, or in a parameter of the network's own naming that the account lists under Personal settings › Advanced › Match conversions by ad click id (user_pref_click_id_params on PUT /users/{id}/preferences, `p202 user prefs update --user-pref-click-id-params`, `bin/p202 user:prefs:update --user-pref-click-id-params`), whose value is tried as every kind of id among that account's clicks only. Pass the id to the network in a sub id slot ([[gclid]] in the campaign URL) and have it sent back. The conversion is recorded only when exactly one click visited in the last 30 days carries the id, compared exactly (case included): two clicks carrying it, or ids naming different clicks, are answered 409 and record nothing, and no click is ever chosen between. The click's account's postback authentication applies as it does to a SubID. A SubID that is there is read exactly as before, and one that names no click is still refused. The conversion is recorded with the new match method ad_click_id (GET /conversions?match_method=ad_click_id, the match-methods breakdown, Group Overview, Visitors and both CLIs), which the offline conversion files and the ad platform destinations treat as a click the conversion named, and Setup › Inbound Log says which parameter found the click or why none was found. 202_google gains a key on each ad click id for the lookup; on a large install the upgrade takes longer while they are built. Only gpb.php does this.
