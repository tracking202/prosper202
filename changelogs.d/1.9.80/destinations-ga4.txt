-- New: Destinations send each new conversion to your ad platforms from your own server, starting with Google Analytics 4 (Measurement Protocol). Add one on Setup › Destinations, with `p202 destination` or `/api/v3/destinations`. A conversion is queued for each enabled destination with the conversion itself and sent by the minutely cron job, retried with backoff (8 tries), and every send is in the destination's delivery log with its status and GA4's answer. A test sends a made-up conversion to GA4's validation endpoint, which records nothing. Raw personal data stays on your server: with "send hashed email and phone" on, only SHA-256 hashes of the customer's normalised email and E.164 phone go out. A visitor your privacy setting holds back is not sent (withheld). The API secret is stored encrypted and never shown again.
