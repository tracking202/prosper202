-- Fixed: the overview and tracking charts went blank on servers that send Referrer-Policy: same-origin (or no-referrer): Highcharts' CDN answers 403 to a request with no Referer, which is what the browser sent it under that policy. The Highcharts script tag now carries its own referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin", so the CDN gets the install's origin (never a page's path or query) whatever the page's policy. Checked in Chromium on a page served with Referrer-Policy: same-origin: the script loads with the attribute and is blocked without it.
