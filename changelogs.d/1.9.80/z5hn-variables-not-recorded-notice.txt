-- New: A traffic source account that names another user's traffic source has recorded no custom variable values for its clicks since 1.9.78, with nothing said. The cron job now gives each such user a traffic source of their own, once an hour and without being asked, wherever a copy is right whatever made the mismatch: every other user's account was put on the source after it was last saved. The source, its user's accounts and its variables are not touched; the other user's accounts use the copy, new clicks on them record their custom variable values again, and their reports list their clicks under it after the next cron run. Account › Settings, GET /system/traffic-source-owners (auto_split, auto_split_blocked_by, splits) and p202 system traffic-source-owners show say which sources are split by themselves, which were split in the last 30 days (for which accounts, and when), and which still wait for a decision and why. No source is ever moved without an operator.
