-- Fixed: Google Ads tracking templates that a paid 1.9.56 install handed out (tracking202/redirect/pt.php?t202id=) reached a missing page after upgrading, so those ads' clicks lost their keyword, cost and traffic source. pt.php is back: it records the landing page's click with everything the template carries, then sends Google on to the page. The landing page's script takes that click by its Google click id (gclid) instead of counting a second one, for the same account, page and visitor address, within ten minutes, as paid 1.9.56 did; so a reload of a landing page carrying the same gclid from the same address within ten minutes is also the same click rather than a new one. Auto-tagging must be on. A click that arrives with only an iOS gbraid or wbraid and no gclid is not matched, so it can be counted twice.
app: Fixed: Google Ads tracking templates from paid 1.9.56 installs record clicks again.
-- New: Get Links gives a landing-page tracker a Google Ads final URL (the page itself, with no tracking variables) and a tracking template for parallel tracking. The template ends in &lpurl={lpurl}, which Google requires for templates set above the ad. A link made for a publisher carries the publisher (t202pubid) in its template too, so template clicks credit that publisher.
