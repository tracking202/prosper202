# P0ZN: a cloaked link's public click id no longer names its neighbours.
-- Fixed: A click's public id, which cloaked links (cl.php?pci=) and landing pages' ways out (pci.php?pci=) carry, was the click id with a random digit either side, so anyone holding one link could reach the clicks around it in at most 81 tries: cl.php sent them on to the other click's destination, and pci.php marked the other visitor's click as gone and set its cookie on them. New clicks from dl.php, rtr.php, record_simple.php and record_adv.php now get a 19-digit id: a 1, the click id in twelve digits and a six-digit suffix keyed by a secret the install mints on its first click (202_deployment_secrets, click_public_id), so one click's id says nothing about another's. New ids are all 10^18 or more and old ones are below it, so links handed out before the upgrade keep working and never name a new click. If the key cannot be read, the click gets a random suffix and the failure is logged. Clicks made before the upgrade keep their old ids, which can still be walked among themselves. No schema change.
app: Fixed: A cloaked link can no longer be used to reach other visitors' clicks; links already handed out keep working.
