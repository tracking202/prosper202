-- New: Setup › Inbound Log lists the Shopify refunds that are not recorded while their order's sale is: the ones waiting for Shopify to say a transaction settled (with how long each has waited, and a warning after a day, naming the Transaction creation webhook when it has never arrived) and the ones refused for good. Each has a form to record what it returned, taken from the order's sale and at most what is left of it, so an order that already has a refund can take another, and a button to close it with nothing recorded. An order on more than one click asks which click the refund takes back from. The amount offered leaves out a transaction Shopify reported as failed; a refund Shopify states in another currency than its sale's is not prefilled and is recorded only at an amount stated in its sale's currency. A refund recorded or closed stays so when Shopify sends it again. Recording or closing needs the Update section. The same list and actions are GET /conversions/shopify-refunds with its record and close routes, and `p202 conversion shopify-refunds list`, `record` and `close`. A refund already on file is never listed as not recorded when Shopify sends it again at another amount; a refund still waiting for its order cannot be closed or recorded by hand (the answer says it is recorded when the sale arrives), while one whose sale is gone, or one refused before its order arrived, can be closed; recording or closing a refund by hand also drops the copy held for its currency, which would otherwise be recorded once the account counts in it. A held refund that was settled since (recorded, or closed by hand) is dropped when held notifications are recorded, never recorded again, and one recorded then leaves the list. POST /conversions/shopify-refunds/{id}/record refuses an amount with more than 5 decimals, and `p202 conversion shopify-refunds record` refuses a leading zero (007).
