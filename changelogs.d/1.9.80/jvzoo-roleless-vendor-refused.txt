# JVZoo vendors on the old 1.9.56 URL choose how it is read, and are no longer silently dropped.
-- Fixed: Account › API integrations › JVZoo has a new setting, "How should the 1.9.56 JVZIPN URL (no ?role=) read your notifications?", with "I promote JVZoo products (affiliate)", the default, or "I sell my own product on JVZoo (vendor)". At that URL (tracking202/static/jvzoo.php with no ?role=), a notification is read as the account whose JVZIPN secret key verifies it chose: as a vendor's, the sale is recorded on the subid in the buy link's variables. Read as an affiliate's, a sale, rebill, refund or chargeback with no caffitid (a vendor's) is now refused with a 400 telling you to choose "vendor" there or paste the ?role=vendor URL into the product's JVZIPN URL, so JVZoo retries it and shows the error, instead of being answered as recorded with no click. When two accounts share the key and chose differently, the notification is refused rather than guessed. The ?role=affiliate and ?role=vendor URLs are read as before.
app: Fixed: A JVZoo vendor still on the 1.9.56 URL can choose "vendor" for it on the API integrations page, and a vendor's sale there is no longer lost silently.
