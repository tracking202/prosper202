# 6MNT: Home says when postbacks and pixels are accepted without the account's token.
-- New: Home warns while an account's postback authentication is off, as the 1.9.80 upgrade leaves every existing account: its postbacks and pixels (pb.php, gpb.php, px.php, gpx.php, upx.php) are accepted without its postback token, so anyone who guesses a click id can add conversions that are then sent on to its ad platforms. The warning says how many of the last 7 days' postbacks and pixels came without the token (the counts Account › API Integrations shows), says it is safe to require only when every one of them was seen and carried the token or a signature, and links to Postback authentication there. It is shown to a role that may open API Integrations. A mode that cannot be read shows nothing on Home and is logged; counts that cannot be read leave the warning without them.
app: New: Home warns while your postbacks and pixels are accepted without your postback token, with how many of the last 7 days' came without it.
