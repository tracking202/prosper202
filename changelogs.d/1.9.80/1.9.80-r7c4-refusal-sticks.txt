-- Changed: A visitor who refuses consent is sent to no destination. Once the tracking link, a click through a rotator, the landing page or the conversion's own pixel or postback carries p202_consent other than 1, none of that visitor's conversions goes to GA4, Google Ads or ChatGPT Ads: not the event, not the click ids, not user data. The delivery is listed as withheld with the reason, and sending it again withholds it again. A refusal on the click holds for the whole visit (a request with no p202_consent no longer gives consent back); a later visit that consents is read on its own. A repeat of a conversion that refuses withholds it from then on; a sale that replaces a refused one is withheld too. Reports still count every conversion. Conversions by the API, imports, payment notifications and app installs cannot carry a refusal; rows recorded before the upgrade carry none. Traffic source pixels and postbacks and the Meta Purchase pixel are unchanged. The refusals are stored by the upgrade (202_click_consent_refusals, and consent_withheld on 202_conversion_logs and 202_goal_events).
