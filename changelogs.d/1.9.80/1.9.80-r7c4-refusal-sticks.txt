-- Changed: A visitor's refusal of consent holds for the whole visit. Once the tracking link, a click through a rotator or the landing page's pageview carries p202_consent=0 (or its campaign has identity capture off), a destination with hashed user data on is not sent that visitor's IP address, whatever a later request of the same visit carries: a request with no p202_consent no longer gives consent back. A later visit of the click that consents is read on its own. The refusal is stored with the click (202_click_consent_refusals, made by the upgrade) and deleted with it.
-- Changed: A conversion whose own pixel or postback carries p202_consent=0 is still recorded and sent to your destinations, with its value and click id but with none of the visitor's data: no IP address, no hashed email or phone, no customer id. The refusal is stored on the conversion (202_conversion_logs.consent_withheld, added by the upgrade).
