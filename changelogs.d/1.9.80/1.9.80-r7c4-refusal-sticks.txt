-- Changed: A visitor who refuses consent is sent to no ad platform. Once the tracking link, a click through a rotator, the landing page or the conversion's own pixel, postback or goal event carries p202_consent other than 1, none of that visitor's conversions goes to GA4, Google Ads or ChatGPT Ads, as a destination or in an offline conversion file (download, API, CLI or fetch URL): not the event, not the click ids, not user data. A destination's delivery is listed as withheld with the reason and stays withheld when sent again; a file counts it as left out (refused_consent). Once a click is refused, none of its conversions is sent, whichever visit of the click they credit: a later request with no p202_consent, or a later visit of the click that consents, does not give consent back. Another click starts fresh. A repeat of a conversion or goal event that refuses withholds it from then on; a sale that replaces a refused one, and a goal conversion written again, are withheld too. Reports still count every conversion. Conversions by the API, imports, payment notifications and app installs cannot carry a refusal; rows recorded before the upgrade carry none. Traffic source pixels and postbacks, the Meta Purchase pixel and the conversion webhooks to your own endpoints are unchanged. The refusals are stored by the upgrade (202_click_consent_refusals, and consent_withheld on 202_conversion_logs and 202_goal_events).
