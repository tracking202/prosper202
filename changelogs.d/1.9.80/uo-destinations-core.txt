-- New: A destination is edited on Setup › Destinations. Edit, under each destination, changes its name, settings, the campaigns it sends, GA4 event parameters, Google Ads per-campaign conversion actions, consent signals and credentials; a credential left blank keeps the stored one, and a Google Ads destination stays connected unless its OAuth client changes. Saving makes the conversions waiting for it due at once. Before, the page could only add, switch and delete one, and a refused customer ID, conversion action or token was mended with `p202 destination update` or by deleting the destination, which cancelled what it had queued.
app: New: Edit a destination on Setup › Destinations: settings, campaigns, parameters, per-campaign conversion actions and credentials (a blank secret keeps the stored one).
-- New: Setup › Destinations counts each destination's last 7 days of conversions by status with the commonest reasons the failed and withheld ones were not sent, and opens its full delivery log, paged and filtered by status, each conversion linked to its click in Conversion Logs. Failed and withheld conversions can be sent again with all their tries: one row from the log, or every one of the last 90 days for a destination (`p202 destination resend`, `POST /destinations/{id}/deliveries/resend`); a withheld one is checked again, and one already sent is never sent twice. Before, a conversion that ran out of tries stayed failed after the cause was fixed, and scrolled off the page after 10 newer rows. 202_destination_deliveries gains the key these reads use.
app: New: Destinations show what failed and why, with the full log, and failed or withheld conversions can be sent again after you fix the cause.
-- New: A destination can be sent the conversions recorded before it existed: Send earlier conversions on Setup › Destinations, `p202 destination backfill --days N` or `POST /destinations/{id}/backfill` queues the paid conversions of the last days it has no delivery for (up to 3 for GA4, up to 90 for Google Ads).
app: New: Send a new destination the conversions recorded before it existed (the last 3 days for GA4, 90 for Google Ads).
-- Fixed: Setup › Destinations said a visitor who may be in the EEA or UK is sent when both consent signals are Granted. Under the privacy setting new accounts start with (Enabled for European Traffic) those visitors are withheld whatever is stated. The page now names the privacy setting in force beside the consent fields and in its notes, links to where it is changed, and warns under a destination that states both signals Granted while the setting still holds those visitors back. The guide, the OpenAPI spec and the CLI guide say the same.
app: Fixed: Destinations say which privacy setting is in force and when it holds back EEA/UK visitors whatever consent is stated.
