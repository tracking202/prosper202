-- New: ChatGPT Ads destination. Setup › Destinations › Add ChatGPT Ads (or `p202 destination create --type openai_ads`, or `type: openai_ads` on POST /api/v3/destinations) sends each new conversion from your server to OpenAI's Conversions API, with your pixel id and Conversions API key: one web event under the conversion's transaction id (else P202-<conversion id>), OpenAI's click id (oppref), the landing page without its query string, and the payout in the currency's smallest unit. By default only conversions whose click came from a ChatGPT ad are sent; the scope setting sends every conversion of one traffic source, or every conversion. With hashed user data on, the email and phone are hashed as OpenAI hashes them and the visitor's IP address is sent when the campaign captures identity, the visitor did not refuse consent and the address was not stored masked. OpenAI keeps the first event it gets for a conversion and takes nothing older than 7 days, so a backfill offers 7 days and a refund does not reach it; a visitor who may be in the EEA or UK is not sent, since OpenAI takes no consent signal. A test fire is checked by OpenAI without being saved.
