# A sale in another currency says what to change, and a JVZoo vendor on the 1.9.56 URL is detected.
-- Fixed: A PayKickstart, Shopify or Zaxaa notification in another currency than the account's, which records nothing, is no longer lost. Prosper202 holds what it read of it (the transaction, amount, currency, click and time; never the body, a secret or the buyer), once however often the platform sends it, and its answer and its line on Setup › Inbound Log name the setting (Account › Personal Settings › Account currency) and the currency to set it to. The integration's card on Account › API integrations shows how many are held and in which currency, and once the account counts in that currency offers Record them: each goes through the checks a notification arriving now goes through, for that account only, in the order it arrived, dated when it happened, a refund under the key its own request gave it. Pressing twice records each once; one the checks refuse, as they would arriving now, is held no longer and the page says why. GET /system/integrations reports the same counts (held_notifications), and what JVZoo's 1.9.56 URL received (no_role_url); POST /system/integrations/record-held and `p202 system integrations record-held <integration>` are the card's Record button. 202_held_payment_notifications is new, added by the 1.9.80 upgrade step. Before, the answer named the two currencies only, the card said nothing, and the sale could not be recorded later.
app: Fixed: A sale in another currency than your account's is held, the integration's card tells you which setting to change, and one button records the held sales once you have.
-- Fixed: A JVZoo vendor whose product still posts to the URL 1.9.56 showed (tracking202/static/jvzoo.php with no ?role=) no longer loses a sale until the "vendor" choice is found. A notification there that names no affiliate (no caffitid and no ctransaffiliate) can only be the vendor's, so it is read as one and recorded on the subid in the buy link's variables, whatever the account chose, and the answer says how it was read. The JVZoo card on Account › API integrations counts what that URL received in the last 30 days (read as a vendor's, read as an affiliate's and recorded nothing, refused because accounts sharing the key chose differently) and, when the notifications look like a vendor's, offers one button, "Read the 1.9.56 URL as a vendor's". The choice itself sits under "Upgraded from Prosper202 1.9.56?", closed for an account that URL has sent nothing to. A notification there that names an affiliate and carries no caffitid is still refused, and the ?role=affiliate and ?role=vendor URLs are read as before.
app: Fixed: A JVZoo vendor still on the 1.9.56 URL has sales with no affiliate recorded, and the JVZoo card offers one button to read that URL as a vendor's.
