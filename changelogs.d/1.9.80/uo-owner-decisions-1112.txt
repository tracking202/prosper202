-- Changed: POST /landing-pages/{id}/check (Check my page, `p202 landing-page check`, the MCP tool landing_page_check) answers a page checked in the last 30 seconds from that check, with `fetched: false`, `checked_at`, `fetch_again_in_seconds` and a note, instead of fetching it again, and each API key may have 10 pages fetched a minute, counted by the key and not by any header. Past that the answer is a 429 with Retry-After and a message saying when to try again, nothing is fetched, and the CLI's hint names the wait and the command to run again. A read-only key may still check a page.

One rule answers for Setup › Inbound Log, GET /conversions/shopify-refunds and its record and close routes, and `p202 conversion shopify-refunds`. A role without Setup reaches the refunds from the Shopify card and is shown only them on the Inbound Log page, with no Setup menu and no log.
