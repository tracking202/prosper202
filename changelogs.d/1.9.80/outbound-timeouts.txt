-- Changed: The global postback (gpb.php) and the universal pixel (upx.php) no longer call the traffic source's server-to-server postbacks before answering. They queue them with the conversion and the notification worker (the cron job, every minute) sends them, with the same retries, one row per URL. Before, a slow traffic source could hold the network's postback open for up to 20 URLs at 10 seconds each.
-- Fixed: Requests to my.tracking202.com that had no time limit now have one: the DNI calls (including 202-cronjobs/dni.php), the ClickServer domain and licence calls, the ad library calls, the survey update and the network tagging on Setup. Five premium calls that waited without limit for a connection now give up after 5 seconds. Every outbound request now has a connect and a total time limit.
-- Fixed: When an account has no dash email stored, the page footer asked my.tracking202.com for it on every page load. It now asks once a day per session.
app: Changed: Postbacks and pixels answer right away and traffic-source postbacks are sent in the background, so a slow outside service no longer slows down your pages or postbacks.
