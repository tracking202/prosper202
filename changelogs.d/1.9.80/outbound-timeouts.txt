-- Changed: The global postback (gpb.php) and the universal pixel (upx.php) no longer call the traffic source's server-to-server postbacks before answering. They queue them with the conversion and the notification worker sends them, with the same retries, one row per URL. The worker is the cron job (every minute), which AutoCron and the signed-in pages' cron ping also start; goal and app-install postbacks already went out this way. An install where none of those runs queues these postbacks without sending them. Before, a slow traffic source could hold the network's postback open for up to 20 URLs at 10 seconds each.
-- Fixed: Requests to my.tracking202.com that had no time limit now have one: the DNI calls (including 202-cronjobs/dni.php), the ClickServer domain and licence calls, the ad library calls, the survey update and the network tagging on Setup. Five premium calls that waited without limit for a connection now give up after 5 seconds. Every curl request the install makes now has a connect and a total time limit, and 202-cronjobs/dni.php answers 502 when the request fails.
-- Fixed: When an account has no dash email stored, the page footer asked my.tracking202.com for it on every page load. It now asks once a day per session.
app: Changed: Postbacks and pixels answer right away and traffic-source postbacks are sent in the background, so a slow outside service no longer slows down your pages or postbacks.
