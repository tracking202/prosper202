-- Changed: The global postback (gpb.php) and the universal pixel (upx.php) answer before they call the traffic source's server-to-server postbacks. Before, a slow traffic source could hold the network's postback open for up to 20 URLs at 10 seconds each. The postbacks are queued with the conversion. Under PHP-FPM or LiteSpeed they are sent right after the answer has gone. Otherwise the notification worker sends them: the cron job, which AutoCron and the signed-in pages' cron ping also start. Retries and the one-row-per-URL tracking are unchanged. A worker run now stops claiming rows after 50 seconds, and it skips the rest of a host's rows once a send to that host has failed, so one slow traffic source no longer holds up the others.
-- Fixed: Requests to my.tracking202.com that had no time limit now have one: the DNI calls (including 202-cronjobs/dni.php), the ClickServer domain and licence calls, the ad library calls, the survey update and the network tagging on Setup. Five premium calls that waited without limit for a connection now give up after 5 seconds. Every curl request the install makes now has a connect and a total time limit, 202-cronjobs/dni.php answers 502 when the request fails, and Account › ClickServers says the service could not be reached instead of failing when a ClickServer call times out.
app: Changed: Postbacks and pixels answer right away and traffic-source postbacks are sent in the background, so a slow outside service no longer slows down your pages or postbacks.
