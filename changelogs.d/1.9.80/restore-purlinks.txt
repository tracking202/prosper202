-- New: PurLinks are back. A landing page link that carries [[subid]] or another tracking token now goes straight to the offer with no tracker redirect, as in paid 1.9.56, and the click-out is recorded in the background with a proof scoped to that pageview. Simple and advanced pages, links and forms (GET and POST), links added later, updatePurLink() and dcs.t202DataObj all work. Until the offer's SubID is ready a link uses the tracker's redirect. The SubID is 1.9.56's click-offer value plus a short signature. gpb.php, pb.php, gpx.php, upx.php, the payment notifications (ClickBank, JVZoo, PayKickstart, Zaxaa, Shopify), revenue uploads and Update Subids all accept it and credit its offer; a SubID whose signature does not match records nothing. gpb.php and pb.php still accept 1.9.56 SubIDs. A snippet opts out with p202_purlink=0 (or off, false or no), on landing.php or a 1.9.56 landing.js URL.
app: New: PurLinks are back: landing page links go straight to the offer, and the click-out is still recorded.
-- New: Deferred pixels are back (p202_defpixel=1 on the landing page snippet; a 1.9.56 snippet's defpixel=1 also works). After a PurLink click the landing page fires the traffic source's browser pixels when the click converts (a postback, a payment notification, the API or an upload; not a conversion a thank-you-page pixel already reported): once per conversion, never for a reversed one, and server postbacks are not sent again. A Bot202 Facebook Pixel Purchase fires there only when the visitor may be tracked: the privacy setting allows it, the visitor has not refused consent and the campaign captures identity.
app: New: Deferred pixels fire your traffic source's pixels on the landing page when a PurLink click converts.
-- New: Goal outcomes of PurLink offers can be listed and re-evaluated in the API and the p202 CLI (subject type purlink).
