-- New: PurLinks are back. A landing page link that carries [[subid]] or another tracking token now goes straight to the offer with no tracker redirect, as in paid 1.9.56, and the click-out is recorded in the background with a proof scoped to that pageview. Simple and advanced pages, links and forms (GET and POST), links added later, updatePurLink() and dcs.t202DataObj all work. Until the offer's SubID is ready a link uses the tracker's redirect. The SubID is 1.9.56's click-offer value plus a short signature; gpb.php and pb.php accept it and still accept 1.9.56 SubIDs. A snippet opts out with p202_purlink=0.
app: New: PurLinks are back: landing page links go straight to the offer, and the click-out is still recorded.
-- New: Deferred pixels are back (p202_defpixel=1 on the landing page snippet; a 1.9.56 snippet's defpixel=1 also works). After a PurLink click the landing page fires the traffic source's browser pixels when the click converts by server postback: once per conversion, never for a reversed one, and server postbacks are not sent again.
app: New: Deferred pixels fire your traffic source's pixels on the landing page when a PurLink click converts.
-- New: Goal outcomes of PurLink offers can be listed and re-evaluated in the API and the p202 CLI (subject type purlink).
