-- New: money-loss alerts are listed in the app. Every alert the hourly run raises is written to the account's alert log (202_alert_log, added by the 1.9.80 step) before any channel is tried, with what each channel then did. Home shows the alerts of the last 7 days and Account › Personal settings › Money-loss alerts those of the last 30: when each was first seen, whether its problem is still going on, a link to the page that shows it, and how it was sent or why each channel did not take it (an alert every channel failed for says so after its third try). An account with nowhere to send an alert still has it listed. The log keeps an alert 90 days after its problem was last seen.
app: New: Money-loss alerts are listed on Home and in your alert settings, with how each was sent or why it was not, so an email that never arrives cannot hide one.
-- New: "Send a test alert" on the Money-loss alerts card sends a harmless alert at once to every channel the saved settings switch on, as a real one is sent, and says what each did: Slack took it or why not, whether the server's mail system accepted the email, and whether a webhook was queued. Webhooks receive it as the new event alert.test, on every webhook subscribed to any alert or to every event. A test is not listed as an alert, and an account can send one every 30 seconds.
app: New: "Send a test alert" checks that your money-loss alerts reach you, and says what each channel did.
-- Changed: each money-loss alert links the page that shows its problem: Setup › Inbound Log for conversions that stopped and for refused postbacks (filtered to the refused ones), the campaign for spend with no conversions. Email and Slack messages carry the link when the install's address is known, webhook payloads carry it as url (null otherwise), and the app's list always does. The address is the install's stored tracking domain, and a cron job run from the command line, which cannot tell where the install is served, now uses the path and scheme a signed-in browser last found (kept when Home or the settings card is opened); until then, and on an install with no tracking domain stored, messages carry no link and the settings card says so. The refused-postbacks alert names its reasons in words (bad signature) rather than as codes (bad_signature).

-- Changed: Account › Attribution shows why a conversion, a visitor merge or a model change is failing, under "What failed and why": its tries, the reason the worker recorded and when it is tried next. "Try again now" makes the account's failed rows due and runs the attribution worker once, then says what still fails. The page no longer sends the reader to p202 attribution queue or to a php command for either. The same retry is POST /attribution/queue/retry and p202 attribution retry (attribution:retry in the PHP CLI): it answers how many of each were made due, what became of the run (ran, busy, failed, idle, waiting) and the backlog after. An account may ask once every 30 seconds, and the worker is run only when something was made due.
