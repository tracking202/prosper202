-- New: Destinations upload each new conversion to Google Ads as a click conversion from your own server, with your own developer token and OAuth client: nothing passes through Tracking202. Add one on Setup › Destinations (Add Google Ads), with `p202 destination create --type google_ads` or POST /api/v3/destinations (type google_ads: customer_id, optional login_customer_id, conversion_action and per-campaign campaign_conversion_actions; developer_token, oauth_client_id and oauth_client_secret, stored encrypted and never shown). Connect Google Ads (or `p202 destination connect`, POST /destinations/{id}/oauth/start) opens Google's consent screen and stores the refresh token when Google sends you back to tracking202/setup/destination-oauth.php, which checks a signed state naming you and the destination and expiring after 10 minutes; until then the destination reads `needs_authorisation` and its conversions wait without using their tries. Each upload carries the click's gclid, wbraid or gbraid (one), the conversion time in the account's time zone, value, currency, the order id (the network's transaction id, else P202-<conversion id>), the stated consent and, with hashed data on, the SHA-256 hashes of the customer's email and phone (enhanced conversions for leads). A conversion with no Google click id and no hashed data is withheld; one whose click is under 6 hours old waits for Google to take it; Google's quota errors, timeouts and 5xx answers are retried; what you must mend (a developer token not yet approved, lost access, a wrong customer ID or conversion action) waits without using tries until you fix it and save, so nothing queued meanwhile is lost; its other refusals fail with its error code. The test sends a validate-only upload, which records nothing. Access tokens are minted per run and never stored, and the tokens and the client secret are removed from every stored or shown error. The setup guide in documentation/features/destinations.md covers the developer token, the OAuth client and Google's 2026 changes to API access.
