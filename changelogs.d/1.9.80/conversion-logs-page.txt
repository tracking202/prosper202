-- New: Conversion Logs is back (tracking202/logs/, a tab next to Update for roles with Setup access), at the address the 1.9.56 paid page had. It lists every conversion recorded on the signed-in account, newest first and 50 to a page: subid, campaign, click time, conversion time, the time between them, IP address, type (pixel, postback, upload, API, payment network, goal), transaction ID, payout, whether it was deleted or reverses another conversion, and the user agent. It filters by date range (on conversion time, or on click time as 1.9.56 did), campaign, subid, type and deleted or live. Rows come from the conversion ledger, only the signed-in account's, and payouts are hidden for roles without campaign data access, as on the reports.
