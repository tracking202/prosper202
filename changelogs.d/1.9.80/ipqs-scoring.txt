-- Fixed: Account › API integrations offered IPQualityScore as real-time fraud detection, but nothing ever used the saved key. Now, for an account with a key, the cron job scores each new click's IP address a few minutes after it arrives (never on the redirect), with a 5-second timeout and each address looked up at most once a day per account, and filters a click scoring 90 or more: it shows under Filtered clicks with the reason fraud_score (GET /clicks, p202 click list --filter-reason fraud_score), and the reports count it as filtered. Only the IP address is sent. Visitors from the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and visitors of unknown country, are never sent and not scored. Scores are deleted after 24 hours and with the user. A failed lookup is shown on the card and retried with backoff.
