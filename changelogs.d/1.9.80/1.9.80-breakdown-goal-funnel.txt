# Goal funnels in the breakdown report.
-- New: GET /api/v3/reports/breakdown takes goals, up to 10 goal ids in funnel order (goals=12,13,14), and gives every row its goal funnel: for each step, how many of the row's clicks reached the goal, how many reached it and every step before it, the rate per 100 clicks, the step rate (per 100 of the step before) and the drop-off from the step before. Rows are any breakdown dimension, so one call answers sign-up, trial and first payment by traffic source, account, landing page or c1 for a campaign. The answer names the steps (goals) and gives the funnel over every click the window and the filters select (goal_totals). A click counts a goal through its live outcomes, its own or its PurLink subjects'; a superseded outcome, a second outcome of the same goal and another account's outcome do not count, and install outcomes stay in the app report. A goal list that is not distinct positive ids, more than 10, or a goal that is not the account's is a 422 naming goals. A funnel asks what reading goals asks (the goals:read scope and Setup), and a role without campaign figures sees its counts as null and its rates. p202 report breakdown --goals (and the MCP tool report_breakdown's goals) sends it, showing each step as goal_01_reached, goal_01_rate, goal_01_step_rate and goal_01_drop_off columns under a table or --csv with a note naming the steps and the totals; bin/p202 report:breakdown --goals shows them as columns named by the step's number and goal ("1. Sign-up reached").
app: New: Report breakdowns can show a goal funnel (sign-up, trial, first payment, ...) for each traffic source, account, landing page or c1, with each step's rate and drop-off.
