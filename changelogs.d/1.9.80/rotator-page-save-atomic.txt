The page now saves through the API's rule-set path (RotatorsController::setDefaultAndRules()): the default and every rule are written in one transaction under the redirector's lock, or nothing is. A rule, criterion or destination saved again keeps its id, so click reports still name it, and a removed rule goes with its criteria and destinations. The page now checks what the API checks: a country is written Name(CC) ("US" alone never matched a visitor), a device in lower case, a new weight of at most 100 and a URL as http:// or https://; a refusal says which rule, criterion or destination it is about. The auto-monetizer, which the page stopped offering, is refused as a destination.
