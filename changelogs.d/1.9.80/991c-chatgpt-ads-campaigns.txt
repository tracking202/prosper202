-- New: ChatGPT Ads campaign control through an ad connection: list campaigns, ad groups and ads as OpenAI has them now, with status, budget, review status and serving issues; pause and activate each; set the tracking template OpenAI appends to the ad's URL (by default the one Get Links gives for the ChatGPT Ads source); add conversion event settings to a campaign while keeping the ones it has; and see spend, impressions and clicks by campaign over whole days in the ad account's time zone.
