-- New: Analyze > Publishers shows what each publisher's links did: clicks, click-throughs, leads, income, cost and net for each publisher, and for each campaign under them, over the range the other reports use, with the clicks that came on no publisher link last so the totals are the account's. The same figures are in the API and the CLI: `publisher` is a breakdown dimension and a group level (GET /reports/breakdown?breakdown=publisher, GET /reports/groups?by=publisher,campaign; p202 report breakdown --breakdown publisher), and every report and the click list take publisher_id (--publisher-id), a publisher's user id or `none` for the clicks credited to no publisher. A click re-clicked through a redirector counts once, and a click counts in the account that owns the link. Get Links says where to find the report.
