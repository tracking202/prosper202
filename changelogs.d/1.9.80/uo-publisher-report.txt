-- New: Analyze > Publishers shows what each publisher's links did: clicks, click-throughs, leads, income, cost and net for each publisher, and under each for every link (named by the t202id in it) and campaign, over the range the other reports use, with the clicks that came on no publisher link last so the totals are the account's, and a Download to Excel of the same rows. A click records the link it came on from this version; a publisher's earlier clicks are listed as "Link not recorded" under their campaign, and the page says so. The same figures are in the API and the CLI: `publisher` and `publisher_link` are breakdown dimensions and group levels (GET /reports/breakdown?breakdown=publisher, GET /reports/groups?by=publisher,publisher_link,campaign; p202 report breakdown --breakdown publisher), and every report and the click list take publisher_id (--publisher-id), a publisher's user id or `none` for the clicks credited to no publisher. A click re-clicked through a redirector counts once, and a click counts in the account that owns the link. Get Links says where to find the report.
