-- Fixed: A publisher link (t202pubid) credited its click to any active Publisher of the install, on any admin's link, so anyone could append another publisher's id to any link and have its clicks counted for that publisher. A click now credits a publisher only when the link's account gave them the Publisher role (User management, POST /users/{id}/roles; saving or re-assigning a user who already holds it grants nothing) or made a publisher link for them in Get Links (202_publisher_grants); any other id credits no one and the click is still recorded. The 1.9.81 upgrade grants every pair that could be credited before, so links already handed out keep crediting, and Get Links lists the publishers an account's links credit and can stop crediting one.
