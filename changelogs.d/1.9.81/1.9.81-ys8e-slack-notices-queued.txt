# YS8E: Slack notices are queued and sent off the request, with retries.
-- Changed: Slack notices are no longer sent inside the request that causes them. A page, a failed sign-in or ClickBank's test queues the notice (202_slack_notices) and answers; the minute's cron job sends it, and on PHP-FPM or LiteSpeed the same request does once its answer has gone and the session is saved. A notice that gets no answer, a 408, a 429 or a 5xx is tried again with the traffic-source outbox's backoff, six times in all over about half an hour; one the webhook refuses (a redirect, a 403 or 404, an address the guard does not allow), one whose account no longer has a webhook, and one older than a day are dropped and logged with why, never with the webhook's path. The webhook is the account's own as saved when the notice is sent. Before, a slow Slack held each request for up to its two seconds, and a notice that failed was lost. Between a deploy and its upgrade, when the table is not there yet, a notice is sent at once as before. A deleted account's queued notices are deleted with it.
app: Changed: Slack notices are queued and sent by the cron job (or after the page answers), and retried when Slack does not take them.
