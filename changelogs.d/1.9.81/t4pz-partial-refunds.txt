# T4PZ: a sale takes more than one partial refund, up to what is left of it.
-- Changed: A ClickBank or PayKickstart refund of a sale already partly refunded is recorded as another partial refund, as a Shopify one already was, taking back what it states of what the sale still credits; before, every refund after the first was answered 409 "a sale is reversed once" and the money stayed credited. A refund stating more than is left takes back the rest and is answered 200 naming both amounts, a refund sent again is still a duplicate, and one arriving when nothing is left is answered 409. A JVZoo or Zaxaa refund, told from another only by its body, is still the sale's one reversal.
app: Changed: Each partial ClickBank or PayKickstart refund of a sale is now taken off its payout, up to what the sale earned.
