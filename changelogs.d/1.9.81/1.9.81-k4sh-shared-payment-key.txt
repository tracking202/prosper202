# K4SH: a PayKickstart, JVZoo, Zaxaa or Shopify key that two accounts store.
-- Changed: When more than one account stores the same PayKickstart, JVZoo, Zaxaa or Shopify secret key, a sale is credited to the account whose click it names (the order the keys are read in plays no part, as before), and a notification naming a click in none of those accounts now says so in the plural (how many accounts store the key, never which) instead of "the account whose secret key verified". The server log line names every account whose key verified it. A ClickBank key two accounts store is still refused (409).
