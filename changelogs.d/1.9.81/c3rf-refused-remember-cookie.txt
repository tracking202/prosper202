# C3RF: a remember-me cookie the database refused is cleared on that response.
-- Fixed: A "Keep me signed in" cookie that can no longer sign in is now cleared by the response that refused it: one that is malformed, names no account or an account with no key, does not verify, or names a key that expired, was deleted (a sign-out, a switch-off, a new password, a password reset) or belongs to a user who may not act. It stayed in the browser until it expired, up to 14 days, and every signed-out request from that browser read its key again. A cookie whose read failed (a database error) is kept, so an outage signs nobody out.
