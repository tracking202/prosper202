-- Fixed: An LTV webhook delivery's outcome is recorded only while the delivery is still pending. The dispatcher wrote it by the delivery's id alone, so a run that recorded a failure after another had delivered the same delivery (its claim had lapsed) made the delivered one pending again and it was sent a second time, and a failure recorded late on a delivery already expired or held could count as its last attempt and mark an active webhook dead. A delivered delivery is now final, the late outcome is written to the error log and the row is left as it is.
app: Fixed: An LTV webhook delivery that was delivered is never queued again by a late failure.
-- Fixed: A repeat `cancel` event on a subscription already canceled (answered `"changed": false`) no longer rewrites the row: it moved the subscription to the top of GET /ltv/subscriptions, which lists the newest change first.
-- Changed: Reviving an LTV webhook (PUT /ltv/webhooks/{id}, `p202 ltv webhooks revive`, LTV Settings) acts on a dead webhook only. A webhook whose stored status is neither `active` nor `dead` (the column also allows `disabled`, which nothing in Prosper202 writes) was made active; it is now refused with an error naming the webhook and the status, and nothing changes. `disabled` is no longer listed as a webhook status in docs/openapi.yaml.
-- Changed: The statuses of LTV subscriptions, webhooks and webhook deliveries, and the moves between them, are declared in one place each (`SubscriptionStatus`, `WebhookStatus`, `DeliveryStatus`), every statement that changes one names the status it moves from, and the lists the API, both CLIs and the Subscriptions page offer are read from them. A canceled subscription that the billing system renews or upserts with another status is reactivated, as before.
