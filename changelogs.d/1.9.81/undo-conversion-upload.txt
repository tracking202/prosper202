-- New: A revenue upload can be undone by its batch id. POST /api/v3/conversions/uploads/{id}/undo deletes every live conversion the upload recorded, on the account's own clicks only, and recomputes each click's value, so the click goes back to the upload or postback it had before; reversals of those rows stop netting and their queued postbacks are cancelled or retracted. ?dry_run=1 lists the rows it would remove and each click's value before and after without writing. GET /api/v3/conversions/uploads lists the account's uploads newest first, and the upload log keeps when a batch was undone and how many rows went. In the Go CLI: p202 conversion uploads and p202 conversion undo-upload <batch-id> (dry run first, then a confirmation unless --force).
