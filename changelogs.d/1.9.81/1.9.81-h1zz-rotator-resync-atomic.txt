-- Fixed: A sync that rewrites a redirector's rules on the target now keeps the ids of the target's rules, destinations and criteria it keeps (a rule by its name, or a renamed rule by its criteria and destinations; a destination by its URL, campaign or landing page; a criterion by its type), so the target's existing clicks keep their rule and destination in reports. Every resync gave every rule and destination a new id.
app: Fixed: Syncing a redirector no longer gives its rules on the target new ids, so the target's clicks keep their rule in reports.
-- Changed: PUT /rotators/{id}/rules takes an empty set, {"rules": []}, which removes every rule in one write (it was a 422), and the redirector's own fields (name, default_url, default_campaign, default_lp, and the read-only keys GET answers) beside the set, written with it in one transaction and refused with it in one 422; the answer then carries the redirector as `rotator`. POST /rotators takes `rules`, creating the redirector and its rules together or not at all. Advertised as features.rotator_with_rules; `p202 rotator set-rules` sends both, and names the upgrade when an older server refuses an empty set.
app: Changed: A redirector's rules can be cleared in one API call, and a redirector's settings and rules saved together.
-- Fixed: A sync to a target that advertises features.rotator_with_rules writes a redirector's own fields and its rules in one request, and creates a new redirector with its rules in one request, so a rule the target refuses leaves the target's redirector as it was (or creates none) instead of updating its name and default and then failing on the rules; a source redirector with no rules clears the target's in one write instead of removing them one at a time. A target from before 1.9.81 is still written in two steps, the redirector and then its rules, and a failure in the second is reported as "applied; its rules then failed".
app: Fixed: Syncing a redirector writes its settings and rules together, so a refused rule leaves the target's redirector as it was.
