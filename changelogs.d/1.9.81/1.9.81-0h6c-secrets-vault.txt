-- New: Integration secrets have one sealed store, 202_integration_secrets: each secret is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) under a data key of its own, which is sealed under the key in P202_SECRETS_KEY, P202_SECRETS_KEY_FILE or the P202_SECRETS_KEY_FILE constant in 202-config.php, or under a key kept in 202_deployment_secrets when none is set. A configured key that cannot be read, or a secret sealed with a key the server does not have, is an error naming it, never an empty secret or the database's key instead. Every change is recorded in 202_integration_secret_events, never with the value (documentation/features/integration-secrets.md).
app: New: Integration secrets are kept encrypted, with a key you can keep outside the database (P202_SECRETS_KEY).
-- Changed: The Shopify webhook secret is the first secret in the sealed store: the upgrade to 1.9.81 moves every account's out of 202_users_pref and drops the plaintext column. Replacing it on Account › API integrations can keep the old one verifying for 7 days; GET /system/integrations says when it was set (secret_set_at) and until when the old one is accepted (previous_secret_expires_at), never the secret. A webhook while a stored secret cannot be opened is answered with a failure Shopify retries, not as unverified.
app: Changed: Replacing your Shopify webhook secret can keep the old one working for a week while your store moves over.
