-- Fixed: A traffic-source retraction that was cancelled because its conversion counted again could still be sent afterwards. When the conversion was revived while the cron job was in the middle of sending the retraction, the failed send put the cancelled retraction back in the queue, and its retry reached the traffic source after the correction that had restored the value, leaving the conversion at 0 there. A cancelled postback now stays cancelled whatever a send already under way answers (one that was delivered is recorded as sent), and the correction waits up to a minute for a send that may still be under way, so it cannot arrive first.
