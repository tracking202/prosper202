-- Changed: A visitor who refuses consent (p202_consent) is told to none of your traffic sources either, as destinations and offline conversion files already were: their conversions fire none of the traffic source's pixels (image, iframe, script, raw markup, the Meta Purchase pixel) and send none of its server-to-server postbacks, from gpb.php, upx.php, a goal reached by an event or a PurLink. The refusal is read as destinations read it: on the conversion's own request, on a conversion it replaced, or on any visit, conversion or goal event of its click. The postbacks are kept in the outbox as withheld, with the reason (a new status, in GET /apps/notifications, p202 app notifications and the app's Notifications panel), and one queued before a refusal arrived on a later visit of the click is withheld when the worker reaches it; one already sent stays sent and a retraction of it still goes. A goal's notification answers status not_sent with reason consent_withheld. Conversion Logs mark such a conversion as held back for consent, and their CSV gains a held_back_for_consent column. A conversion whose refusal cannot be read is sent nothing. The cron output counts the postbacks held back.
