-- Changed: Forwarding headers are believed only from a proxy listed in the new P202_TRUSTED_PROXIES environment variable (addresses, CIDR ranges, `cloudflare` for Cloudflare's published ranges, or `none`); with the variable not set, Cloudflare's ranges are trusted, so an install behind Cloudflare alone keeps its visitors' addresses. Before, any visitor could send X-Forwarded-For, CF-Connecting-IP or X-Real-IP and be recorded at the address they chose, and X-Forwarded-Proto set the Secure flag of the session and p202vid cookies. Now the client is the connecting address unless that is a listed proxy, whose X-Forwarded-For or RFC 7239 Forwarded header is read from the right past listed hops (both present and naming different visitors: the connecting address), so the click's address, its GEO and ISP, the duplicate-IP filter, the sign-in throttle and the public endpoints' rate limits all key on an address the visitor cannot choose, and behind a listed proxy each visitor has its own rate-limit bucket. CF-Connecting-IP and the other single-value headers are no longer read. A value that cannot be read, `none`, or a set but empty variable trusts no proxy; an unreadable one is logged at most hourly. Upgrading: an install behind a load balancer or a reverse proxy other than Cloudflare records the proxy's address for every visitor until its proxies are listed, and Account › Settings and the signed-in health check now say so, naming the address (the Coolify stacks list Cloudflare and the private Docker ranges); a TLS-terminating proxy's X-Forwarded-Proto counts only once it is listed or the web server sets HTTPS itself, as the Docker image does.
app: Changed: Visitor addresses and HTTPS behind a proxy come only from Cloudflare or proxies listed in P202_TRUSTED_PROXIES. Behind a load balancer or another proxy, list it (Settings warns, and the FAQ says how), or every click is recorded at the proxy's address.
