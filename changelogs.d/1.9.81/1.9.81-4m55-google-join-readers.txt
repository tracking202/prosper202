# 4M55: every reader of a click's gclid and UTM values reads one 202_google row per click.
-- Fixed: A click's gclid and UTM values are read once per click, and from one row, wherever Prosper202 reads them: the report rollup, the traffic source pixels' tokens, the gpb.php and upx.php postbacks, the tracker URL's tokens, the Visitors download, the offer rotator's recorded link and parallel tracking's lookup. 202_google's key is (click_id, gclid), so a click can have more than one row there (no version writes a second today), and each of these joined the table: the Visitors download listed such a click once per row, the postbacks and tokens took whichever row came first, and the rollup kept whichever came last. Now the row read is the one with the greatest gclid, the last in the table's own key, the same in every reader: its UTM source, medium, campaign, term and content are read together, never one row's source beside another row's medium, and its gclid is the greatest of the click's, as GET /clicks and the offline conversion export already read it. The other ad click ids (gbraid, wbraid, ttclid, msclkid, fbclid, oppref) are still each the greatest of the click's rows, as before. A click with one row reads exactly as before.
