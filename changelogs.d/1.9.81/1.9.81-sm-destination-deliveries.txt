# Destination deliveries: the status is one lifecycle (DeliveryStatus), written by one class (DeliveryRows).
-- Fixed: Destinations (Setup › Destinations, and /api/v3/destinations): deleting a destination while the cron job was sending one of its conversions could leave that delivery "pending" for good, or "failed". The delete cancelled the row and the send, when its answer came back, wrote its own result over the cancellation; a pending row of a deleted destination is never read again, so it stayed waiting and was counted as waiting. A send now writes its result only to a row that may still take it: a delivery cancelled meanwhile stays cancelled when the send did not deliver, and is recorded as sent when the platform did take it.
app: Fixed: A conversion being sent to a destination while you deleted it could stay "waiting" for good. It is now cancelled, or shown as sent if the platform took it.
-- Fixed: Destinations: a delivery already recorded as sent could be put back to waiting, and sent a second time, when a send of the same row that had outlived its try (a platform that took longer to answer than the retry backoff) came back with a failure after a later run had delivered it. Sent is now final, and a send that did not deliver writes nothing once a later run has claimed the row.
-- Fixed: Destinations: a delivery could be left "pending" for a deleted destination, where nothing read it again. Deleting a destination cancels the rows it can see, and a row could become pending after that: a conversion whose recording transaction had read the destination before the delete committed queued its row afterwards, and a delivery withheld for having no currency was released to pending when the owner recorded one, whether or not its destination still existed. The cron job's send now reads the pending rows of deleted destinations too and cancels them.
-- Changed: Destinations: every write of a delivery by its id now also names its account.
