-- New: GET /api/v3/me answers who is signed in and what they may see and do, in one read: the user, the account's time zone and currency, the roles, every role permission and flags derived from them (is_admin, can_see_money, can_edit_campaigns and others, each narrowed by the key's scope where the route behind it asks for one), the licence and sponsored-ads switches, the install's code and schema versions (which GET /system/version answers to admins only) and the feature list GET /capabilities advertises. Any valid key reads it, whatever its scope or role. documentation/api/30-me.md describes every field.
