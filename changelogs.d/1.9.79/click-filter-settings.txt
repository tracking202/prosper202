# Click filtering settings, the reason a click was filtered, and the MCP tools for them.
-- New: You choose how clicks are filtered (Personal Settings › Click filtering; GET and PUT /users/{id}/click-filter; p202 click-filter). Each of the three checks that mark a click filtered can be switched off: a click from an address a user of the install last signed in from, a click from the built-in search engine crawler ranges, and a repeat click from one address, whose window is 1 hour, 6 hours, 24 hours (the default, as before) or 7 days. An address list of up to 500 IPv4 or IPv6 addresses or CIDR blocks marks each one always counted (a client's office that tests your links; it wins over every check) or always filtered (a click farm; it wins over the three checks), compared with the address a click arrives from, before privacy masks it. Test an address on the page, with GET /users/{id}/click-filter/test or with p202 click-filter test, to see whether a click from it would be filtered and by which rule. An account that changes nothing is filtered exactly as before, and bot detection always applies

-- New: A filtered click says why: GET /clicks/{id} and GET /clicks answer filter_reason (sign_in_address, crawler_range, repeat_visit, filter_list with the list's filter_entry_id, or bot), as p202 click get and click list do, and GET /clicks takes filter_reason to list the clicks one rule filtered (p202 click list --filter-reason; not_recorded lists the filtered clicks that have no reason). A click filtered before 1.9.79 has no recorded reason and answers null, except a bot click, which answers bot

-- New: An AI assistant connected through p202 mcp reads and changes the click filter (click_filter_get, click_filter_update, click_filter_test, click_filter_list_add, click_filter_list_remove) and reads the account's settings (settings_get: GET /users/{id}/preferences' answer, a secret only as whether one is stored). Tracking202's hosted MCP server does not offer them, since its connections do not reach account settings; click_get answers filter_reason on both
